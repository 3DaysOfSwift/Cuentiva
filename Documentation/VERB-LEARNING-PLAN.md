# Next feature: Yesterday, today, tomorrow

Plan only. No verb game or additional purchases are implemented by this work.

Build short, character-led situations around the same action in three time frames: today I order a coffee, yesterday I ordered one, tomorrow I will order one. Begin with present, preterite and ir a + infinitive; introduce imperfect and simple future separately, with context that makes the intended meaning clear.

Use reviewed structured data: infinitive, tense, person, regional form, correct conjugation, bilingual example, accepted alternatives, explanation and source book/scenario. Explicitly handle accents, irregulars, ser/ir sharing preterite forms, omitted subject pronouns, and Latin American ustedes versus vosotros. Do not infer correctness from an unrestricted word pool.

Proposed sequence:
1. Choose yesterday/today/tomorrow from a short scene.
2. Replace the verb to move the sentence through time.
3. Build the sentence, gradually removing hints.
4. Revisit difficult forms on a later day.

Keep scheduling, answer validation, progress and rewards in the shared model; keep each exercise UI modular. Persist attempts by verb, tense and person rather than interpreting book completion as CEFR mastery. Use familiar book characters and the café/bus scenarios.

Before implementation: review example content with a fluent Spanish editor, decide accepted regional variants and reward rules, then test distractors, accent handling, irregular forms, resume behaviour and accessibility. Ship one small reviewed set before expanding.
