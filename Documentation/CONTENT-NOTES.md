## Documentary and health sources

*Después del documental* is a fictional family discussion, not a fictionalization of Joe Cross's biography. It briefly summarizes the real film's reported personal transformation and 60-day juice fast. It does not treat those reports as controlled evidence, claim a cure, or encourage readers to repeat the fast or change medication. The script was prepared from the official synopsis and health references, not a full viewing or transcript analysis.

- [Joe Cross: official film descriptions](https://www.rebootwithjoe.com/watch-here/): documentary premise and the 60-day juice fast. The site itself notes that results vary and the regimens are not intended to cure disease.
- [NCCIH: Detoxes and Cleanses](https://www.nccih.nih.gov/health/detoxes-and-cleanses-what-you-need-to-know): limited evidence, restrictive-diet nutritional concerns, and the need to discuss significant changes with a health professional.
- [NIDDK: Eating and Physical Activity](https://www.niddk.nih.gov/health-information/weight-management/adult-overweight-obesity/eating-physical-activity): sustainable eating and activity habits.
- [Harvard Nutrition Source: Lectins](https://nutritionsource.hsph.harvard.edu/anti-nutrients/lectins/): raw/undercooked bean risks and the role of preparation. The plant story does not endorse broad lectin avoidance or present a cooking-time recipe.

## Financial sources

## Vocabulary and publication

Every Spanish surface word has an occurrence count and a curated lemma or surface-form fallback. This remains a lightweight index, not a contextual morphological analysis; ambiguous words such as “trabajo” cannot represent multiple lemmas in the current single-entry schema. Proper names and the English documentary title remain surface forms. These limitations should be addressed before vocabulary-driven recommendations claim linguistic precision.

The new texts are original demo content. They are not verified community memoirs, direct adaptations of the named books, or medical/financial advice tailored to a reader.
