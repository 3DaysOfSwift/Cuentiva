# Cuentiva — 15 new stories

Original fictional microbooks. Approximate A1–B1 editorial levels. Native-speaker review and production licensing remain pending. The documentary discussion has fictional characters but references a real film; it distinguishes testimony from medical evidence.

## Después del documental — After the documentary (B1)

Presenta su pérdida de peso y su relato de una mejoría de salud.

It presents his weight loss and his account of improved health.

También muestra cómo su experiencia anima a otras personas a cambiar.

It also shows how his experience encourages other people to change.

Julia se siente inspirada, pero su padre hace una pregunta.

Julia feels inspired, but her father asks a question.
