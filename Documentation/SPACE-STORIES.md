# Three space stories

Fictional characters learning factual science. Provisional A2/B1 levels; native-speaker review pending.

## Los pies en la Tierra — Feet on Earth (B1)

A playground question separates gravitational attraction from rotation.

En el parque, Alba pregunta por qué no salimos volando si la Tierra gira.

In the park, Alba asks why we do not fly off if Earth spins.

Su padre deja caer una pelota y le habla de la gravedad.

Her father drops a ball and tells her about gravity.

En la descripción de Newton, los objetos con masa se atraen entre sí.

In Newton’s description, objects with mass attract one another.

La atracción depende de la masa y de la distancia, no solo del tamaño.

The attraction depends on mass and distance, not just size.

Cerca de la superficie terrestre, apunta aproximadamente hacia el centro del planeta.

Near Earth’s surface, it points approximately toward the planet’s center.

Toda la Tierra contribuye a esa atracción, no únicamente su núcleo.

The whole Earth contributes to that attraction, not only its core.

El giro produce un efecto centrífugo aparente que reduce un poco nuestro peso, sobre todo en el ecuador.

The spin produces an apparent centrifugal effect that slightly reduces our weight, especially at the equator.

Pero ese efecto es mucho menor que la atracción gravitatoria.

But that effect is much smaller than gravitational attraction.

La rotación actual no basta para lanzarnos al espacio, y el suelo nos sostiene.

Earth’s current rotation is not enough to launch us into space, and the ground supports us.

Alba recoge la pelota y mira el planeta que tiene bajo los pies.

Alba picks up the ball and looks at the planet beneath her feet.

## El viaje del Sol — The Sun’s journey (A2)

A planetarium visit reveals the journey of our whole solar system.

En el planetario, Mateo ve la Tierra dar vueltas alrededor del Sol.

In the planetarium, Mateo sees Earth going around the Sun.

Entonces pregunta si el Sol también viaja.

Then he asks whether the Sun travels too.

La guía muestra una imagen de nuestra galaxia, la Vía Láctea.

The guide shows an image of our galaxy, the Milky Way.

El Sol orbita alrededor del centro de esa galaxia.

The Sun orbits the center of that galaxy.

Los planetas lo acompañan en el viaje.

The planets accompany it on the journey.

Una vuelta completa tarda unos doscientos treinta millones de años.

One complete orbit takes about two hundred and thirty million years.

El Sol también gira sobre sí mismo, pero ese es otro movimiento.

The Sun also spins on its own axis, but that is a different motion.

Mateo descubre que girar y recorrer una órbita no significan lo mismo.

Mateo discovers that spinning and following an orbit do not mean the same thing.

Al salir, se sienta en un banco y mira el cielo.

On leaving, he sits on a bench and looks at the sky.

Parece estar quieto, pero sigue viajando con todo el sistema solar.

He seems to be still, but he is still traveling with the entire solar system.

## Un planeta sin suelo — A planet without ground (B1)

Jupiter challenges a learner’s picture of a gas giant.

Irene dibuja una nave que quiere aterrizar en Júpiter.

Irene draws a spacecraft that wants to land on Jupiter.

Su profesora le pregunta dónde pondría las patas.

Her teacher asks where it would put its landing legs.

Júpiter es un gigante gaseoso, pero no está hecho únicamente de gas.

Jupiter is a gas giant, but it is not made entirely of gas.

Está compuesto principalmente de hidrógeno y helio.

It is composed mainly of hydrogen and helium.

En sus regiones profundas, la enorme presión transforma el hidrógeno en un fluido denso.

In its deep regions, enormous pressure turns hydrogen into a dense fluid.

Más abajo, parte de ese hidrógeno se comporta como un metal líquido.

Farther down, some of that hydrogen behaves like a liquid metal.

No hay una superficie sólida como el suelo de la Tierra donde podamos aterrizar.

There is no solid surface like the ground on Earth where we could land.

Los datos de la misión Juno sugieren un núcleo difuso, mezclado con el material que lo rodea.

Data from the Juno mission suggest a diffuse core, mixed with the material around it.

Saturno también es un gigante gaseoso, y su interior tampoco es solo gas.

Saturn is also a gas giant, and its interior is not just gas either.

Irene cambia su dibujo: ahora la nave observa el planeta desde una órbita.

Irene changes her drawing: now the spacecraft observes the planet from orbit.

## Scientific sources and scope

- [NASA: What is gravity?](https://spaceplace.nasa.gov/what-is-gravity/en/): introductory gravitational attraction. The story explicitly uses Newton’s description; it is not a complete account of general relativity.
- [NASA: The rotating Earth](https://pwg.gsfc.nasa.gov/stargaze/Srotfram1.htm): rotation slightly reduces effective weight, with the largest effect at the equator. It does not create gravity or currently fling surface objects into space. Earth’s whole mass contributes, not only the core; surface attraction points approximately toward its center.
- [NASA: Sun facts](https://science.nasa.gov/sun/facts/): the Sun and solar system orbit the Galactic center in roughly 230 million years. Axial rotation and orbital motion are distinct. The story does not imply the Sun circles outside the galaxy or that only its central black hole supplies the galactic gravitational field.
- [NASA: Jupiter facts](https://science.nasa.gov/jupiter/jupiter-facts/): composition, interior pressure, and lack of a solid landing surface.
- [NASA: Juno](https://science.nasa.gov/mission/juno/): evidence for a diffuse core and metallic hydrogen; internal structure remains an active research topic.
- [NASA: Gas giant interiors](https://science.nasa.gov/resource/gas-giant-interiors-2003/): Saturn also has dense fluid layers. Used for that general distinction, not the older illustration’s precise core model.

A gas giant is not a planet composed solely of matter in a gaseous state. The books deliberately avoid presenting a definite solid core or an exact interior boundary as established. Vocabulary retains the existing curated-lemma/surface-fallback limitations.
