## Description
Bring Spanish to life through stories.

Step into Cuentiva's illustrated library of short Spanish stories, with English translations to help you follow along. Meet curious storytellers, discover today's books and build a collection of completed reads.

Read a sentence, listen to it, then practise speaking or writing. Explore friendly explanations of language terms and keep track of your vocabulary and reading streak.

On devices where Apple Intelligence is available, turn a memory into a private fantasy tale with your own storyteller. Publish one saved tale to your personal library every seven days. Your stories stay on your device.

Complete a new book to earn one doubloon, then spend it on a continuous Spanish conversation with a storyteller. Each new chat session costs one doubloon; coins cannot be purchased. It requires a supported device with Apple Intelligence available.

Try the introductory book, then subscribe for $9.99 per month or $39.99 per year, billed yearly (US pricing; regional prices vary). Both auto-renewing plans provide the same library access while active. Payment is charged to your Apple Account; subscriptions renew unless cancelled at least 24 hours before the period ends. Manage or cancel in your App Store account settings. New library downloads require an internet connection.

## Review notes — verify against the submitted build

No account or sign-in is required. The introductory café book is available without the library purchase. The monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions unlock the same wider collection while active. Restore purchases is available in Settings and on the paywall.

Storyteller Chat costs one earned doubloon per session, charged on the first successful response. Completing a distinct book earns one doubloon. Leaving the chat screen ends the session. No separate chat purchase or coin purchase is offered. Open a storyteller biography or the chat entry in Settings. Generation uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models and requires Apple Intelligence to be available. Test on a supported physical device with Apple Intelligence enabled; the app explains unavailable states.

Write creates private local tales. There is no public upload or publishing platform. “Publish to my library” adds one saved tale per seven days to the user's own Discover collection.

Microphone access is requested for speaking practice. Spanish recognition requires on-device support and offers writing as a fallback. Cuentiva does not request location access.
