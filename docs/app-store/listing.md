## Description
Bring Spanish to life through stories.

Step into Cuentiva's illustrated library of short Spanish stories, with English translations to help you follow along. Meet curious storytellers, discover today's books and build a collection of completed reads.

Read a sentence, listen to it, then practise speaking or writing. Explore friendly explanations of language terms and keep track of your vocabulary and reading streak.

On devices where Apple Intelligence is available, turn a memory into a private fantasy tale with your own storyteller. Publish one saved tale to your personal library every seven days. Your stories stay on your device.

Storyteller Chat is an optional, separate one-time purchase for Spanish conversations with the characters. It requires a supported device with Apple Intelligence available.

Try the introductory book, then unlock the library with a one-time purchase. There is no recurring subscription. Downloaded books remain available offline; new library downloads require an internet connection.

## Review notes — verify against the submitted build

No account or sign-in is required. The introductory café book is available without the library purchase. The library non-consumable unlocks the wider collection. Restore purchases is available in Settings and on the paywall.

Storyteller Chat has a separate non-consumable entitlement. Open a storyteller biography or the chat entry in Settings. Generation uses Apple's on-device Foundation Models and requires Apple Intelligence to be available. Test on a supported physical device with Apple Intelligence enabled; the app explains unavailable states.

Write creates private local tales. There is no public upload or publishing platform. “Publish to my library” adds one saved tale per seven days to the user's own Discover collection.

Microphone access is requested for speaking practice. Spanish recognition requires on-device support and offers writing as a fallback. Nearby optionally requests location and uses Apple's location/place services. The current nearby stories are explicitly fictional examples.
