## Data on your device

Cuentiva stores your reading progress, vocabulary, practice history, storyteller profile, private stories and chat conversations on your device. It does not provide a public story-submission service or user accounts. The app does not directly sync this data using CloudKit. Your device's backup settings may include app data in an Apple device backup.

During upgrades, older local data files are removed after successful migration to the current database. If migration fails, the original data remains available for a retry.

## Speech and AI

Speaking practice uses supported on-device Spanish speech recognition. The app does not save microphone recordings or upload them to our servers. If on-device recognition is unavailable, you can use writing practice instead.

Private story and chat generation use Apple's on-device Foundation Models when available. Cuentiva does not send your prompts, biography or conversations to an external AI service.

## Network services

Cuentiva downloads library content from GitHub. These requests expose ordinary connection information, such as your IP address, to the delivery service. Personal stories and reading progress are not included in library requests.

Apple handles purchases and purchase verification through the App Store. Cuentiva does not receive your payment-card details.

Cuentiva does not request your location. Older local drafts may retain location metadata saved by an earlier version; this remains on your device.

## Your choices

You can change microphone permission in iOS Settings. Cuentiva offers a learning-progress reset in its settings; that is not a deletion of every story or conversation. Removing the app removes its local app storage, subject to device backups managed by Apple. Contact us about information you have sent directly for support.
