## Purchases

The library offers monthly and annual auto-renewable subscriptions. Manage or cancel through your App Store account settings. Storyteller Chat uses earned doubloons, not a separate purchase. To recover a purchase, use Restore purchases in Settings while signed in to the Apple Account used to buy it. Apple manages billing and refund requests.

## Apple Intelligence

Private story generation and Storyteller Chat require a supported device with Apple Intelligence available. If generation is unavailable, follow the message shown in the app. Reading the existing collection does not require AI generation.

## Your stories and progress

Your storyteller, private stories, chats and reading progress are stored locally. Cuentiva does not provide an account-based sync or recovery service. Deleting the app can remove this data; device-backup recovery depends on your Apple backup settings.

## Reading and speaking

Downloaded books can be read offline. New collection downloads need an internet connection. Speaking practice needs microphone permission and supported on-device Spanish recognition; writing practice is available as an alternative.
